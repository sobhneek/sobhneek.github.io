I am an MSc Candidate in the Health Systems Research Program at the Institute of Health Policy, Management and Evaluation at the University of Toronto.

My thesis attempts to evaluate the necessity of social risk adjustment in Ontario's case-mix system. Ultimately, I hope the findings of this work can help inform future iterations of patient-level funding reforms for hospitals.


## Research Interest
- Hospital funding
- System stewardship
- Case costing
- Hospital financial health
- Case-mix systems
